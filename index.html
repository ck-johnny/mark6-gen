Mark Six Generator

Version 1.1.0

Generate unique sorted Mark Six numbers offline. Switch between one custom draw and multiple 6-number sets.

  • Number mode: generate X non-repeating numbers from 1–49. The count is optional and defaults to 7.
  • Set mode: generate X sets, each with 6 non-repeating numbers from 1–49. Pick 2, 10, or enter your own count.
  • Results are sorted and displayed three numbers per row.
